Delete your Folio account

Folio - Daily Life Journal, by Animesh Singh

How to request deletion

  1. Email animeshsingh0704@gmail.com from the Google email address you use with Folio.
  2. Use the subject "Delete my Folio account".
  3. We will confirm your request and complete deletion within 30 days.

What gets deleted

Your account details (name, email, profile photo and Google ID), your synced journal entries, moods, badges, your backed-up photos, videos and voice notes, and your notification and sign-in tokens.

What may be kept

Limited server logs may remain for up to 30 days. Google Play keeps its own purchase records, and we may keep purchase records where the law requires it.

Before you delete

  • Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in Google Play first.
  • Data on your phone stays there until you uninstall Folio or clear its data.
