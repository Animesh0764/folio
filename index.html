Folio

A daily life journal that feels like paper.

Folio gives you a calendar of your whole year and a freeform page for each day. Place notes, photos, post-its, mood stamps, voice notes and stickers anywhere on the page, build a writing streak, and lock entries away as time capsules to open later.

Private by default

Your journal is saved on your phone first. When you are signed in, your entries sync to our server so you can get them back. We do not sell your data or show ads. Read the privacy policy.
