Privacy Policy

Effective 4 November 2026

This policy explains what the Folio app ("Folio", "we") collects and what we do with it. Folio is developed by Animesh Singh, based in India. Contact: animeshsingh0704@gmail.com.

What we collect

DataWhy
Google account details: name, email address, profile photo, Google account IDTo sign you in and identify your account
Journal content: the text you write, moods, stickers, page layout, dates, word counts, capsule lock dates, streaks and badgesTo sync and back up your journal
Purchase information: subscription status, expiry date and a purchase IDTo give you the features you paid for
Technical data: IP address, request details and error logsTo run the service, prevent abuse and fix problems
Notification token, if notifications are usedTo deliver notifications to your device

Photos, video and voice notes

Folio asks for access to your photos and microphone only so you can add them to a page. In the current version these files stay on your device and are not uploaded to our servers. Because of this, they are not backed up: you can lose them if you uninstall the app or change phones. If we add cloud backup for media, we will update this policy first.

Who we share data with

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or use your journal to train AI. We share data only with the service providers that run Folio:

  • Google for sign-in and Google Play purchases
  • Supabase for database hosting
  • Render for server hosting
  • RevenueCat for managing subscriptions
  • Expo for delivering notifications

We may also disclose information if the law requires it. Our providers may process data outside your country, including in India and Singapore.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS, and sign-in tokens are kept in secure storage on your device. Your journal is not end-to-end encrypted, which means it could technically be accessed on the server. We do not read your entries except where needed to fix a problem you report or where the law requires it. No system is perfectly secure.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while your account is active. To delete your account and its data, follow the steps on the delete account page. We complete deletion within 30 days of confirming your request. Data on your phone stays there until you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, or ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing animeshsingh0704@gmail.com. You can turn off notifications in your phone settings at any time, and you can raise a complaint with us at the same address.

Children

Folio is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it. Anyone under 18 should use Folio with a parent or guardian's permission.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and tell you in the app if the change is significant.
